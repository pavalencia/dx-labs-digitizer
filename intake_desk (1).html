AI Edition — you are the human in the loop

An AI reads each messy travel document and fills in the data. Your job is to check its work before it goes into the system — approve the good reads, fix the misreads, and flag the ones the AI only guessed.



HOW TO PLAY
✔ APPROVE
The AI read every field correctly. Send it through.
✏ NEEDS FIX
The AI misread a field (wrong number, date, or name). Choose this, then tap the wrong field to earn a bonus.
🚩 FLAG
The document is unreadable or missing info, and the AI guessed. Don't let it through — flag it for a human.

⚡ Faster judgments score more. 🔥 Correct calls in a row build a combo. 🎯 Catch a confident-but-wrong AI read for a big bonus. Never rubber-stamp a bad read — that's how bad data gets in.
